The Key Takeaway

Please make sure your rating is clear. Here is the final summary:

1. SUCCESS & OPTIMIZATION? (Give 5 Stars)

If the training was effective, valuable, and met your goals, please give 5 stars. **You can still provide comments** for minor improvements (like content modifications or training duration) without lowering this score.

2. FUNDAMENTAL FLAW? (Give 4 Stars + Comment)

A 4-Star rating signals a training that **fell short due to a trainer's fundamental skill issue** (e.g., technical deficiency, poor clarity). If you choose 4, you **must add a detailed comment** explaining the core skills that were missing. This high effort confirms your choice.

3. FAILURE? (Give 3 Stars or Less + Comment)

If the course was inadequate or unhelpful, give 3 stars or lower and please add a comment to explain the issues.
